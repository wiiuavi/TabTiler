Important Notes & Limitations

⚠️ Tab Reloading: When you convert a standard tab into a tiled pane, the webpage must reload. Any unsaved data (like filled forms or playing videos) will be reset.
⚠️ Cross-Origin Dragging: Due to Chromium security restrictions, you cannot drag and drop images or selected text from one pane into another if they are different websites.

How to use Extract: If you need more space for a specific pane, use the ⏏ (Extract) button in the popup menu. This pulls the tab out of the grid and opens it as a standard Chrome tab, leaving the grid slot empty for a new URL.
